How to Merge PDF Files Online for Free

Need to combine several PDF documents into one file? Merging PDFs can make it easier to share paperwork, submit assignments, and keep related office documents together. This guide explains a simple way to do it using Office Toolbox.

What does merging PDF files mean?

Merging PDFs means combining two or more PDF files into a single PDF document. For example, you might combine several scanned pages, a report and its appendix, or separate documents that need to be shared together.

How to merge PDF files in 5 steps

  1. Open the free PDF merger. Visit the Office Toolbox Merge PDF tool.
  2. Select your PDF files. Choose at least two PDF files from your device. The tool currently supports up to 20 files and a total upload size of 100 MB.
  3. Check the file order. Review the selected files. Move files up or down to set the order you want in the final document.
  4. Preview and merge. Check the files and click the merge button to combine them. If a file is password-protected or invalid, you may need to unlock it or choose another copy first.
  5. Download the result. Save the merged PDF and open it to confirm the pages are in the correct order.
Tip: Give your files clear names before uploading, and check the final PDF before sending it to a client, college, or colleague.

When can merging PDFs be useful?

  • Office work: Combine a report with supporting pages or appendices.
  • Students: Put related assignment pages into one document.
  • HR and administration: Organize related forms and paperwork into a single file when your process permits it.
  • Everyday tasks: Combine receipts, manuals, or reference documents for easier sharing.

Is it safe to merge employee or personal documents online?

Be careful with sensitive information. Before uploading identity documents, bank details, salary records, or confidential company files, check your organization's rules and the tool's privacy information. If you are unsure, use an approved offline PDF application instead. Never upload documents you do not have permission to process.

You can read the Office Toolbox Privacy Policy before using the tool. The website's hosting providers may process technical request information, so do not treat any online service as a guarantee of zero data retention.

Common problems and quick fixes

The PDF will not upload

Confirm that the file is a PDF, that it opens on your device, and that your selected files fit within the tool's current limits.

The pages appear in the wrong order

Return to the file list and move the PDFs into the correct order before merging again.

The PDF is password-protected

You may need an authorized, unlocked copy. Only remove protection when you have permission to do so.

The merged file will not open

Try downloading it again and opening it in a current PDF reader. If the issue continues, check whether one of the original PDFs is damaged.

Not necessarily. Merging combines documents; it does not automatically compress their contents.
