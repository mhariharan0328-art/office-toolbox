Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Office Toolbox handles information when you visit our website or use our free PDF and employee-document tools.

Effective date: 18 October 2026

1. About Office Toolbox

Office Toolbox provides online productivity tools, including PDF merging and employee-document packing. This policy applies to the Office Toolbox website at mhariharan0328-art.github.io/office-toolbox/ and its associated online tools.

2. Information You Provide

Depending on the tool you use, you may provide files such as PDFs, images, or employee-related documents. The Employee Document Packer may also request an employee name, employee ID, or document categories.

Please upload only files you are authorized to use. Employee documents may contain sensitive personal information. Avoid uploading confidential information unless you understand and accept the risks of using an online processing service.

3. How Uploaded Files Are Processed

Our tools send uploaded files to our backend service when server-side processing is required. For example, the PDF Merge tool sends PDFs to the service to combine them into a single PDF.

Our tools are designed to process files temporarily rather than maintain a permanent document-storage service. Temporary working files are intended to be deleted after processing is completed.

The resulting file is returned to you for download. We do not intend to use your uploaded documents for advertising or sell their contents.

Important: Temporary-file deletion does not guarantee immediate erasure from every technical system. Hosting providers may process operational logs or retain technical records according to their own policies. Do not treat Office Toolbox as a secure document vault or long-term storage service.

4. Information Collected Automatically

Our website does not currently have Google Analytics installed. However, the website and its hosting providers may process technical information needed to deliver pages, maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and operate the service.

Depending on the provider and request, technical records may include IP addresses, browser information, request timestamps, and error details. We do not control all records that hosting providers may generate or retain.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Office Toolbox does not currently use Google Analytics. We do not intentionally use analytics cookies to track visitors at this time.

Our hosting providers or third-party resources may use technical mechanisms necessary for delivery, security, or operation. The use of such technologies depends on the relevant service and its configuration.

6. Hosting and Third-Party Services

Office Toolbox uses third-party services to operate its website and tools. These currently include:

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website's frontend files.
  • Render: hosts the backend service that processes uploaded documents.
  • Third-party content delivery services: may deliver resources such as the PDF preview library used by the Merge PDF page.

These providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies, terms, and retention practices. Please review their policies for details about their handling of information.

7. Advertising

Office Toolbox does not currently display advertisements and has not yet enabled Google AdSense.

If advertising is introduced in the future, this policy will be updated to describe relevant advertising technologies, cookies or identifiers, and available privacy choices where applicable.

8. How We Use Information

Information submitted through our tools is used as necessary to:

  • Provide the processing function you requested.
  • Return the resulting file to you.
  • Maintain the reliability and security of the service.
  • Investigate technical errors or misuse when necessary.

We do not intend to sell personal information or use the contents of uploaded documents for targeted advertising.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps within our control to operate the tools responsibly. However, no online service or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Please keep a copy of your original documents and download any output you need. Do not upload files unless you are comfortable with the information being transmitted to the service for processing.

10. Children's Privacy

Office Toolbox is intended for general productivity use and is not specifically directed at children. Please do not submit children's personal information unless you are authorized to do so and have an appropriate legal basis.

11. Your Privacy Choices

You can choose not to use the tools or not to upload a file. If you have a question or request concerning personal information handled through Office Toolbox, contact the site operator using the contact details published on the website.

Requests involving records held by GitHub, Render, or another provider may need to be directed to that provider, depending on the records involved.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our tools, hosting arrangements, analytics, or advertising practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when appropriate.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the Office Toolbox site operator using the contact information published on our website.

Before publishing: Add a working contact email address or create a contact page so visitors have a clear way to reach you.
